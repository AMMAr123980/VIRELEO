SaaS MVP Development Cost Summary (2026)

In 2026, building a production-grade custom SaaS Minimum Viable Product (MVP) typically costs between $4,500 and $12,000, with an average delivery timeline of 4 to 8 weeks. A lean micro-SaaS with one core workflow and Stripe billing starts at $4,500 – $6,500, standard multi-tenant B2B platforms cost $7,500 – $11,000, and advanced AI-powered SaaS platforms range between $9,000 and $14,000. The final price depends on architectural complexity, third-party API integrations (such as AI models or payment webhooks), user permission hierarchies, and whether you hire a high-overhead US agency ($30k–$80k+) versus an agile specialist engineering studio ($4.5k–$12k).

Real Cost Tiers for SaaS MVP Development in 2026

A production-ready SaaS product is fundamentally different from a marketing website. It requires user authentication, database state management, subscription billing webhooks, and transactional emails. Based on over 40+ completed software builds, custom SaaS MVPs naturally divide into three verified tiers:

1. Micro-SaaS Platform Passwordless OAuth, single problem solver workflow, Stripe Checkout, simple user dashboard. 3 – 4 Weeks $4,500 – $6,500
2. Standard B2B SaaS Multi-tenant database, team roles & permissions, Stripe customer portal, analytics, automated email alerts. 5 – 7 Weeks $7,500 – $11,000
3. AI-Powered SaaS Platform LLM inference pipeline (OpenAI/Claude), vector search, background processing queues, token usage tracking. 6 – 8 Weeks $9,000 – $14,000

Custom Code vs No-Code Builders (Bubble, FlutterFlow)

Many first-time founders ask whether they should launch their MVP on no-code platforms like Bubble. While no-code is adequate for non-transactional directories or disposable mockups, building database-driven SaaS on no-code introduces major strategic liabilities:

  • Zero Code Export: You cannot export Bubble code. If their pricing triples or their server goes down, you have zero recourse.
  • Database Latency: Heavy visual builder DOM trees and non-indexed queries cause slow API response times, frustrating early paying users.
  • Investor Valuation Penalty: Sophisticated angel investors and technical acquirers heavily discount SaaS startups that do not own their intellectual property and code repository.

Frequently Asked Questions About SaaS MVP Costs

How much does it cost to build a SaaS MVP in 2026?

In 2026, building a custom, production-grade SaaS Minimum Viable Product (MVP) typically costs between $4,500 and $12,000 with a delivery timeline of 4 to 8 weeks. A focused micro-SaaS starts at $4,500–$6,500, a standard multi-tenant B2B platform costs $7,500–$11,000, and an AI-powered SaaS platform ranges between $9,000 and $14,000.

What are the ongoing monthly infrastructure costs after launch?

A modern lean SaaS MVP built on modern cloud services typically incurs $45 to $75 per month in operating expenses: Vercel/Cloudflare hosting ($20/mo), Supabase/Neon PostgreSQL database ($25/mo), Resend transactional email ($0–$15/mo), and Stripe fees (2.9% + 30¢ per transaction only when revenue is generated).

Do I own 100% of the source code after development?

Yes, absolutely. Upon milestone completion, 100% of the intellectual property, private GitHub repositories, database schemas, CI/CD pipelines, and cloud accounts are transferred to your company with zero recurring agency license fees or proprietary platform lock-in.

Why is custom code better than building on No-Code tools like Bubble?

Custom-coded SaaS apps (built with Next.js, Node.js, and PostgreSQL) offer zero vendor lock-in, sub-second response times, full IP ownership for investors, predictable cloud hosting costs, and unlimited scalability. No-code tools frequently impose severe database query limits, vendor lock-in, and steep tiered pricing once user traffic increases.

How much does an AI-powered SaaS MVP cost compared to standard software?

An AI-powered SaaS MVP typically costs between $9,000 and $14,000. The additional engineering covers OpenAI/Claude API integration, streaming responses, vector database embeddings (Pinecone/pgvector), asynchronous worker queues, and prompt token caching.

Can I build a SaaS MVP for under $5,000?

Yes — if the scope is tightly focused. A micro-SaaS with a single core workflow (such as a document generator, lead scraper, or single-purpose dashboard) with passwordless auth and Stripe billing can be engineered for $4,500 to $5,500 in 3–4 weeks. The key is strict scoping: build only the single high-value workflow needed to sign up paying early adopters.
