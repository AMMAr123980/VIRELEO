Frequently asked questions

Questions about AI automation.

What is AI workflow automation?

AI workflow automation combines business rules, software integrations and, where useful, AI models to assist with repeatable tasks such as classifying enquiries, summarising information or routing requests. The exact design depends on data quality, integrations and review requirements.

Can AI automation work with my existing tools?

Often, if the tools provide supported APIs, webhooks or other reliable integration methods. Integration access, permissions, costs and platform limitations should be checked before implementation.
