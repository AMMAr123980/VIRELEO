Frequently asked questions

A quick guide to VIRELEO’s digital engineering services and how a project can get started.

What services does VIRELEO provide?

VIRELEO provides custom Website development, web applications, mobile app development, AI workflow automation, AI chatbots, and messaging automation for WhatsApp and Instagram. The right solution depends on your goals, users, and existing tools.

Can VIRELEO build a custom website or web application?

Yes. VIRELEO can help plan and build business websites, interactive websites, e-commerce experiences, web applications, and SaaS MVPs. Scope, features, timeline, and budget should be agreed before development begins.

What is AI workflow automation?

AI workflow automation connects steps in a business process so repetitive work can be reduced. Depending on the use case, this may include processing incoming information, routing requests, updating connected tools, preparing summaries, or assisting a team with routine tasks.

Can VIRELEO create AI chatbots for websites or messaging channels?

VIRELEO offers AI chatbot and messaging-automation solutions for website support and channels such as WhatsApp and Instagram. The bot’s capabilities depend on the available business information, platform access, integrations, and the approval rules required for the project.

How do I start a project with VIRELEO?

Use the Contact page to explain your business, the problem you want to solve, the features you need, and any target deadline. A useful first discussion clarifies scope, integrations, priorities, and the next steps.

Does VIRELEO guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No responsible provider can guarantee a specific Google ranking. VIRELEO’s website can be prepared with sound technical SEO, clear service information, crawlable pages, and structured data, but rankings depend on many factors including competition, content quality, authority, and how search engines index the site.
