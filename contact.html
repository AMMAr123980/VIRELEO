Direct answers

Frequently asked questions about working with VIRELEO.

Everything you need to know about project timelines, communication channels, NDA agreements, and engagement models.

What information should I prepare before contacting VIRELEO?

Share a brief overview of your product idea, target audience, core features or required integrations (such as Stripe, WhatsApp API, or OpenAI models), and your desired launch timeline. A high-level concept is enough to initiate our discovery review.

How quickly does VIRELEO respond to project inquiries?

We review and respond to all project inquiries sent via our discovery form, email, or WhatsApp within 24 business hours with initial technical feedback and scheduling options for an architecture call.

Does VIRELEO sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?

Yes. We routinely sign mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) prior to reviewing proprietary project specifications, database architectures, or business workflows to safeguard founder intellectual property.

What engagement models and payment terms do you offer?

We operate primarily on transparent milestone-based fixed-price contracts for custom websites, SaaS MVPs, and mobile applications. For ongoing automation maintenance and iterative feature sprints, we provide dedicated monthly engineering retainers.

Do you collaborate with international clients across different timezones?

Yes. We routinely work with startups and businesses across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, the UAE, and Saudi Arabia. We schedule synchronized weekly video sprint demos and maintain asynchronous communication via Slack, WhatsApp, and private GitHub issue boards.
